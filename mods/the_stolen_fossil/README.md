# The Stolen Fossil

A Quest System-compatible investigation quest for Gen1Recomp.

## Requirements

- Gen1Recomp 0.1.38 or newer.
- Quest System 1.0.3 or newer.

## Quest flow

1. Earn the Boulder Badge.
2. Speak to the front-desk museum guide, who is reachable from the main entrance without Cut.
3. Question a museum witness and two Pewter City residents.
4. Follow the evidence to Mt. Moon.
5. Discover a Team Rocket purchase receipt.
6. Defeat a thief on Mt. Moon B1F.
7. Stop the Rocket buyer on Mt. Moon B2F.
8. Return the fossil and choose a level 10 Omanyte or Kabuto.

## Quest System integration

- The quest appears in the QUESTS journal after the Boulder Badge is earned.
- Objectives, locations and progress update after every clue and battle.
- Quest markers identify the current investigation NPC.
- The front-desk guide receives the available and turn-in markers.
- The journal records the selected reward and marks the quest as completed.

## Installation

Place both the `quest_system` and `the_stolen_fossil` folders in the game's `mods` directory. Enable them from the in-game Mod Manager. Quest System is a required dependency and loads first automatically.

## Notes

The quest giver is the front-desk guide at Pewter Museum's main entrance. Accepting the quest waives the museum admission fee, so the first witness is immediately accessible.

The quest uses existing Pewter Museum, Pewter City and Mt. Moon maps. It does not replace ROM-derived assets. The custom opponents use the standard Team Rocket battle portrait with original quest-specific parties. If the party and every PC box are full, make room and speak to the front-desk guide again before choosing the reward.
