# The Empty Throne

A fully English quest mod for Gen1Recomp, unlocked after obtaining the Earth Badge.

## Requirements

- Gen1Recomp 0.1.38 or newer
- Quest System 1.0.3 or newer

## Quest flow

1. Obtain the Earth Badge and return to Viridian Gym.
2. Speak to the Gym Guide after Giovanni has disappeared.
3. Descend through the hidden lift beneath Giovanni's empty throne.
4. Defeat the Vault Warden and open the first security door.
5. Decide whether to free or abandon imprisoned Rocket recruits.
6. Decide whether to preserve the Rocket succession archive as evidence or destroy it.
7. Survive two or three consecutive elite Rocket battles without a healing break.
8. Enter the throne room and defeat Commander Vesper's complete six-Pokemon team.
9. Decide whether to arrest Vesper or accept his offer to dissolve the remaining Rocket cells.
10. Return to the Gym Guide and claim the recovered rewards.

## Moral choices

- **Free the recruits:** fully heals the party and removes the Rocket Enforcer from the consecutive gauntlet.
- **Keep them locked:** adds the Rocket Enforcer as an additional consecutive battle.
- **Save the archive:** preserves evidence and changes one member of Vesper's final team to Porygon.
- **Burn the archive:** prevents the research from being reused and changes Vesper's team to his personal battle roster.
- **Arrest Vesper or take the deal:** changes the final outcome recorded by the quest.

## Commander Vesper

Vesper uses a complete team ranging from level 55 to level 60. His final roster changes according to the archive decision, but both versions contain six high-level Pokemon and end with Rhydon and Dragonite.

## Reward

- A level 45 Porygon with maximum Gen 1 DVs and Stat Exp: 15 DV and 65,535 Stat Exp in every statistic.
- One Master Ball.

## Notes

- The Viridian Command Vault is a new map supplied by the mod.
- The elite gauntlet provides no automatic healing between battles.
- Freeing the recruits is the only moral route that heals the party inside the vault.
- Opened security doors and completed decisions remain saved after leaving or losing a battle.
- Porygon and the Master Ball are tracked independently, so a full party, full Boxes or full Bag cannot permanently lose either reward.
