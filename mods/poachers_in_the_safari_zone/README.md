# Poachers in the Safari Zone

A fully English quest mod for Gen1Recomp, unlocked after obtaining the Soul Badge.

## Requirements

- Gen1Recomp 0.1.38 or newer
- Quest System 1.0.3 or newer

## Quest flow

1. Obtain the Soul Badge.
2. Speak to the ranger inside the Safari Zone Gate.
3. Leave the normal party safely with the ranger and enter a restricted reserve with a temporary Nidorino.
4. Follow muddy tracks and survive a falling-net trap.
5. Defeat a poacher, free Rhyhorn and add it to the temporary reserve team.
6. Use tall grass to avoid the first patrol.
7. Survive an electric snare, defeat a trapper and free Doduo.
8. Use Doduo's cry to distract the second patrol.
9. Reach the hidden camp and defeat the Poacher Leader using the rescued Safari Pokemon.
10. Return to the ranger and choose a rare perfect Pokemon.

## Temporary team rule

The player's original party is copied into protected mod save data before the operation. Inside the reserve it is replaced by a ranger Nidorino, then rescued Rhyhorn and Doduo join it. The original party is restored unchanged when the player leaves, wins the operation, blacks out or loads outside the reserve.

## Reward

Choose one level 35 Pokemon:

- Kangaskhan
- Tauros
- Scyther
- Pinsir

Every reward has maximum Gen 1 DVs and Stat Exp: 15 DV and 65,535 Stat Exp in every statistic.

## Notes

- The operation uses a new map supplied by the mod.
- Safari Balls, the normal Safari timer, Cut, Surf and Flash are not required.
- Trainer battles prevent captured poacher Pokemon from being caught.
- The reward remains available when the party and every PC Box are full.
