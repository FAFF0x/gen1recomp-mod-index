# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Added Poachers in the Safari Zone after the Soul Badge.
- Added the Safari Zone Gate ranger as a Quest System-compatible quest giver.
- Added a custom restricted Safari reserve with tracking, traps and two stealth patrol routes.
- Added a protected temporary-party system that stores and restores the player's original party.
- Added ranger Nidorino and rescued Rhyhorn and Doduo as temporary battle allies.
- Added three custom poacher trainer battles.
- Added a reward choice between Kangaskhan, Tauros, Scyther and Pinsir.
- Added maximum DVs and Stat Exp to every reward Pokemon.
- Added Quest System journal, objectives, progress and markers.
