# The Abandoned Cabin

A fully English quest mod for Gen1Recomp, unlocked after obtaining the Thunder Badge.

## Requirements

- Gen1Recomp 0.1.38 or newer
- Quest System 1.0.3 or newer

## Quest flow

1. Obtain the Thunder Badge.
2. Speak to the sailor near Vermilion Harbor.
3. Investigate the old trail sign beside Diglett's Cave on Route 11.
4. Follow the signal after dark and enter the abandoned cabin.
5. Defeat the electrified security Pokemon.
6. Restore the three generators in the RED - BLUE - RED sequence.
7. Enter the hidden Team Rocket laboratory.
8. Defeat the Rocket Engineer's enhanced Magneton.
9. Return to the sailor and receive the rescued Electabuzz.

## Boss

A level 30 Magneton with maximum Gen 1 DVs and Stat Exp, using Thunderbolt, Swift, Supersonic and Thunder Wave.

## Reward

A level 30 Electabuzz with maximum Gen 1 DVs and Stat Exp: 15 DV and 65,535 Stat Exp in every statistic.

## Notes

- The cabin is a new map supplied by the mod.
- Cut, Surf and Flash are not required.
- The dark palette and dialogue represent nighttime exploration without requiring a real-time clock.
- A wrong generator cable resets the full generator sequence.
- The reward remains available when the party and every PC Box are full.
