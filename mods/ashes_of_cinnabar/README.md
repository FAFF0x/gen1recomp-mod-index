# Ashes of Cinnabar

A fully English quest mod for Gen1Recomp, unlocked after obtaining the Volcano Badge.

## Requirements

- Gen1Recomp 0.1.38 or newer
- Quest System 1.0.3 or newer

## Quest flow

1. Obtain the Volcano Badge.
2. Speak to Blaine in Cinnabar Gym after the island tremor.
3. Use his emergency tunnel to reach a burning laboratory beneath Pokemon Mansion.
4. Explore a three-sector scorched laboratory while the ventilation timer counts down from 180 steps.
5. Recover three secret documents containing the security codes.
6. Open three permanent security doors.
7. Survive ruptured steam lines, chemical fires and collapsing ash vents.
8. Defeat artificial Porygon, Muk and Magneton specimens.
9. Reach Project MIMIC and battle a Ditto core that cycles through three forms copied from the player's current party.
10. Return to Blaine and receive Arcanine.

## Environmental timer

- Each laboratory entry begins with 180 steps.
- Warnings appear at 120, 60 and 20 remaining steps.
- Wrong security codes consume 15 steps.
- Fire hazards consume 10 steps and damage one party member without causing a faint.
- At zero, the emergency system ejects the player to Cinnabar Gym.
- Recovered files, defeated specimens and opened doors are preserved between attempts.

## Boss

Project MIMIC is represented by one unstable Ditto core with three transformation phases. At the beginning of the battle, the mod selects up to three Pokemon from the player's current party. The three enemy phases copy those species, statistics and moves; copied moves receive five PP, matching Gen 1 Transform behavior.

The boss is a Trainer-style encounter, so it cannot be caught or escaped from.

## Reward

A level 42 Arcanine with maximum Gen 1 DVs and Stat Exp: 15 DV and 65,535 Stat Exp in every statistic.

## Notes

- The laboratory is a new map supplied by the mod.
- Cut, Surf, Strength and Flash are not required.
- The reward remains available when the party and every PC Box are full.
