# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Added the Ashes of Cinnabar quest after the Volcano Badge.
- Added Blaine as the Quest System-compatible quest giver.
- Added a custom burning laboratory beneath Pokemon Mansion.
- Added a 180-step environmental ventilation timer with warnings and emergency ejection.
- Added three secret documents and three permanent security doors.
- Added fire hazards and artificial Porygon, Muk and Magneton encounters.
- Added Project MIMIC, a three-phase Ditto boss that copies the player's party forms.
- Added a level 42 Arcanine reward with maximum DVs and Stat Exp.
- Added Quest System journal, objectives, progress and markers.
