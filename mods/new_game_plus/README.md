# New Game Plus

A post-League challenge mode for Gen1Recomp that preserves the existing save and adds repeatable high-level content.

## Unlock and activation

After defeating the Champion, `NEW GAME+` appears in the Start menu. Activating it preserves the Pokédex, party, inventory, money, badges, story progress and boxes.

## Features

- Every ordinary trainer receives a large level increase and eligible Pokémon evolve.
- Natural wild encounters gain levels while retaining their original species and probabilities.
- Eight Gym Leader rematches with full teams of six.
- Three sequential optional bosses: Blue Prime, Dragon Master and Red Echo.
- First-win money and item rewards in every cycle.
- Repeatable cycles; each new cycle adds another five levels to trainers and rematches.
- Progress is stored in the mod's private save namespace.

## Controls

Open Start Menu -> NEW GAME+. Use A to select, B to return, and Left/Right to jump through the long list.

## Compatibility notes

The mod does not reset story flags. It is a safe postgame NG+ mode rather than a second story playthrough. Link battles are unaffected. Encounters created directly by utility mods such as SUMMON are not treated as natural map encounters and therefore keep the level chosen by those mods.
