# FREE RARE CANDY

Adds **RARE CANDY** to every standard Poké Mart and sets its purchase price to **¥0**.

## What it changes

- RARE CANDY appears in the normal **BUY** list of every standard Poké Mart.
- Purchase price is **¥0**.
- Buying candy does not reduce the player's money.
- Uses the game's normal ShopMenu and Bag logic.
- No save migration and no new save are required.
- Compatible with UI mods that restyle the normal shop because this mod does not replace ShopMenu.

## Notes

Gen1Recomp's stock shop quantity selector calculates its initial maximum from the player's money even when an item's price is zero. The purchase itself is still free. With very little or no money, the selector may initially allow fewer candies per transaction; you can simply make additional free purchases.
