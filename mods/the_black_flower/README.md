# The Black Flower

A fully English quest mod for Gen1Recomp, unlocked after obtaining the Rainbow Badge.

## Requirements

- Gen1Recomp 0.1.38 or newer
- Quest System 1.0.3 or newer

## Quest flow

1. Obtain the Rainbow Badge.
2. Speak to Erika in Celadon Gym.
3. Enter Celadon's secret garden through the passage behind the Gym.
4. Navigate a living plant maze filled with black spores and dead ends.
5. Defeat a possessed Parasect, Tangela and Weepinbell.
6. Each victory destroys a corrupted root and reveals a hidden path.
7. Reach the black clearing and defeat the possessed Victreebel.
8. Return to Erika and receive Vileplume.

## Boss

A level 38 Victreebel with maximum DVs, boosted Stat Exp and a custom moveset: Razor Leaf, Sleep Powder, Wrap and Acid.

## Reward

A level 35 Vileplume with maximum Gen 1 DVs and Stat Exp: 15 DV and 65,535 Stat Exp in every statistic.

## Notes

- The secret garden is a new map supplied by the mod.
- Cut, Surf and Flash are not required.
- Spore fields can poison one healthy party member, but each field activates only once.
- Hidden hedge paths open permanently as the corrupted roots are cleansed.
- The reward remains available when the party and every PC Box are full.
