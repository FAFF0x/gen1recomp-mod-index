# Whispers Beneath Cerulean

A fully English quest mod for Gen1Recomp, unlocked after obtaining the Cascade Badge.

## Requirements

- Gen1Recomp 0.1.38 or newer
- Quest System 1.0.3 or newer

## Quest flow

1. Obtain the Cascade Badge.
2. Return to Cerulean City and speak to the woman training her Pokemon.
3. Enter the maintenance hatch leading to the Cerulean Drainage Channels.
4. Defeat contaminated Poliwag, Goldeen and Psyduck.
5. Activate all three water-control valves.
6. Defeat or catch the contaminated Seaking in the final reservoir.
7. Return to the canal keeper for the reward.

## Reward

A level 20 Starmie with maximum Gen 1 DVs and Stat Exp: 15 DV and 65,535 Stat Exp in every statistic.

## Notes

- The canal dungeon is a new map supplied by the mod.
- Cut and Surf are not required.
- Leaving through the entrance returns the player to the exact position used to enter.
- The reward remains available when the party and every PC Box are full.
